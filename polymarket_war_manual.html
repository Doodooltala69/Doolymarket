THE EXECUTION

  1. Monitor: Track all crypto threshold markets (BTC > $X, ETH > $Y, etc)
  2. Watch: In the final 60 seconds before market closes, watch BOTH real price and Polymarket price
  3. Detect: When real price crosses threshold but Polymarket hasn't adjusted yet
  4. Execute: Buy the correct side immediately (< 1 second)
  5. Wait: Market closes, you win

STRATEGY 3: MATHEMATICAL ARBITRAGE

Why This Third: Zero risk. Guaranteed profit. But small returns and rare opportunities.

THE SETUP

In a binary market, YES + NO should equal $1.00 (plus fees). Sometimes it doesn't. When YES + NO + fees < $1.00, you can buy BOTH sides and guarantee profit.

THE MATH

Market: "Will event happen?" YES price: $0.46 NO price: $0.48 Polymarket fee: ~2% Total cost: $0.46 + $0.48 = $0.94 With fees: $0.94 × 1.02 = $0.9588 One side MUST pay $1.00 Profit: $1.00 - $0.9588 = $0.0412 per dollar Return: 4.12% risk-free

THE EXECUTION

  1. Scan: Check all markets for YES + NO price
  2. Calculate: Add prices + fees
  3. Detect: If total < $0.98, opportunity exists
  4. Execute: Buy BOTH YES and NO simultaneously
  5. Wait: Market resolves, you get paid

FREQUENCY & SIZING

  • Opportunities: 1-4 per day
  • Win Rate: 100% (literally guaranteed)
  • Avg Return: 2-8% per trade
  • Position Size: 80-100% of available capital (zero risk)
  • Time to Profit: Hours to days (until market resolves)

USE THIS AS PARKING LOT

When you're not actively trading Strategy 1 or 2, park your capital here. It's earning 2-8% risk-free while you wait for high-ROI opportunities.

STRATEGY 4: VOLATILITY SCALPING

Why This Fourth: Requires experience. Higher risk. But can produce 50-150% returns repeatedly.

THE SETUP

Markets panic. A bad poll drops, price crashes 30% in 5 minutes. You buy the panic. Price recovers 20-40% over next few hours. You sell. Repeat.

PATTERN RECOGNITION

Classic Panic-Recovery Pattern:

  1. Market is stable at X price
  2. Bad news / uncertainty → Price crashes 20-50%
  3. Panic sellers exit → Price at bottom
  4. Rational buyers enter → Price recovers 30-70% of the drop
  5. You buy at bottom, sell on recovery

REAL EXAMPLE

Market: "Candidate A wins election" Normal price: $0.62 10:00 AM: Bad poll released 10:05 AM: Panic selling, price at $0.34 ← BUY HERE 12:30 PM: Market stabilizes at $0.52 ← SELL HERE Bought at: $0.34 Sold at: $0.52 Profit: $0.18 per share = 53% return in 2.5 hours With $5,000: You make $2,650 profit

HOW TO IDENTIFY BOTTOMS

  • Volume spike: Lots of trades happening (panic phase)
  • Price stabilizes: Stops dropping, bounces slightly
  • Orderbook depth: Buy orders start appearing
  • Time: Usually bottoms 3-10 minutes after bad news

RISK MANAGEMENT

  • Only trade 15-25% of capital (this can go wrong)
  • Set stop loss: If price drops another 10% after you buy, exit
  • Take profits early: Don't wait for full recovery, sell at 40-60% bounce
  • Don't catch falling knives: Wait for price to stabilize before buying
PORTFOLIO APPROACH: Run Strategy 1 (crypto lag) + Strategy 2 (news) as primary profit drivers. Use Strategy 3 (math arb) as passive income. Add Strategy 4 (volatility) only when you have experience and spare capital.
