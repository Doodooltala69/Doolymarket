💡 Core Concept

Polymarket is a prediction market where people bet on real-world events. Like any market, it has inefficiencies we can exploit:

  • Price Delays: There's a lag between real market prices (like crypto) and Polymarket's updates
  • Math Errors: Sometimes YES + NO shares don't equal $1, creating guaranteed profit opportunities
  • Information Gaps: We can act on breaking news before the market adjusts
  • Cross-Market Differences: Same events priced differently across platforms

🎯 Trading Strategies

Four proven methods to extract profit from Polymarket

💡 Strategy Priority: Start with #1 (lowest risk), then add #2 (high frequency), then #3 and #4 as you scale

💎 Strategy #1: Probability Sum Arbitrage

The Safest Strategy - START HERE

How It Works: When YES + NO prices don't add up to $1, buy both sides for guaranteed profit.

Example:

  • YES share: $0.48
  • NO share: $0.48
  • Total cost: $0.96 + fees ($0.02) = $0.98
  • Guaranteed payout: $1.00
  • Net Profit: $0.02 (2% return, risk-free)

⚡ Strategy #2: Crypto Price Lag

High Speed, High Profit

How It Works: 3-10 second delay between real crypto prices and Polymarket. Trade before market catches up.

Example:

  • BTC hits $100,300 at 11:59:55 PM
  • Market "Will BTC close above $100k?" still at 42% YES
  • Buy YES @ $0.42
  • Market closes at 12:00 AM with BTC at $100,300
  • Win $1.00 → 138% profit!

📡 Strategy #3: Early Information

News Before the Crowd

How It Works: Monitor news/social media, detect market-moving events before mainstream, trade before crowd reacts.

⚠️ Higher Risk: Requires NLP and careful analysis. Start small!

🔄 Strategy #4: Cross-Market Arbitrage

Multi-Platform Edge

How It Works: Same event priced differently on Polymarket vs Kalshi/sportsbooks. Buy low, sell high, lock in profit.

✓ Risk-Free: Hedged positions = guaranteed profit
